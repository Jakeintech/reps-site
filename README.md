# reps-site

Public legal and support site for [Reps](https://apps.apple.com/) — the strength-training app where your iPhone sees your form, an NFC tap logs your set, and nothing ever leaves your device.

## Pages

- [index.html](index.html) — landing
- [privacy-policy.html](privacy-policy.html) — full privacy policy (GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, COPPA, PIPEDA, Apple 5.1.3 HealthKit)
- [terms-of-service.html](terms-of-service.html) — full ToS (Apple EULA incorporation, subscription terms, arbitration, injury waiver)
- [health-disclaimer.html](health-disclaimer.html) — health and safety disclaimer
- [support.html](support.html) — FAQ and contact
- [data-deletion.html](data-deletion.html) — data-deletion instructions (no account; step-by-step)
