Health & Safety Disclaimer

Last updated: April 18, 2026

Reps is a fitness and strength-training tool, not a medical device. It is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease, injury, or health condition.

The exercise guidance, form analysis, rep counting, AI coaching notes, longevity metrics, and all other content and features provided by Reps are informational only and are not a substitute for professional medical advice, physical therapy, or supervision by a qualified fitness or healthcare professional.

1. Consult a physician before you lift

Strength training is a physical activity that carries inherent risk. Before beginning any exercise program — including any routine, program, or recommendation surfaced by Reps — you should consult a licensed physician, especially if any of the following apply to you:

  • You have or have had a heart condition, high blood pressure, or a family history of cardiac events.
  • You have a musculoskeletal injury, joint condition, or chronic pain.
  • You are pregnant, postpartum, or nursing.
  • You are recovering from surgery or a serious illness.
  • You are over 40 and have been sedentary.
  • You take medication that affects heart rate, blood pressure, balance, or coordination.
  • You have any other condition that could be aggravated by resistance training.

2. Stop and seek help when something is wrong

Stop exercising immediately and seek medical attention if you experience any of the following during or after using Reps:

  • Chest pain, pressure, or tightness
  • Dizziness, lightheadedness, or fainting
  • Shortness of breath disproportionate to effort
  • Sharp or sudden joint, muscle, back, or tendon pain
  • Nausea, cold sweats, or disorientation
  • Numbness or tingling in any limb
  • Any other symptom that feels wrong

If you believe you are having a medical emergency, call your local emergency number (911 in the US) immediately.

3. Form analysis is not a substitute for a trainer

Reps' on-device computer-vision form analysis estimates joint angles, bar path, tempo, and asymmetry from a single camera view. It is an aid, not a diagnosis. Camera angle, occlusion, clothing, and lighting all affect accuracy. A low form score does not mean you are injured; a high form score does not mean your lift is safe. Always defer to a qualified coach or physical therapist for injury-risk assessment.

4. AI coaching content

On supported devices, Reps uses Apple's on-device Foundation Models to generate coaching notes, weekly summaries, and natural-language feedback. This content is AI-generated, runs entirely on your device, and is not reviewed by a human before being shown. AI output may be incomplete, inaccurate, or inappropriate for your specific condition. Do not rely on AI-generated coaching to make medical, rehabilitative, or competitive-programming decisions.

5. HealthKit and longevity metrics

Reps reads mobility, gait, heart, and sleep metrics from Apple HealthKit to display a "Longevity Panel." These metrics are trends, not clinical measurements. They are not intended to diagnose frailty, cardiovascular risk, recovery status, or any medical condition. Speak with your physician about what these numbers mean for you.

6. Menstrual cycle periodization

If you opt in to cycle-aware periodization, Reps reads HealthKit menstrual data to suggest intensity adjustments. These suggestions are based on general training literature, not personalized medical guidance. They are not a contraceptive, a fertility tracker, or a substitute for gynecological care.

7. Assumption of risk

By using Reps, you acknowledge that strength training involves risk of injury, including but not limited to muscle strain, ligament or tendon tears, joint injury, dropped weights, equipment failure, and in rare cases, serious or fatal cardiac events. You voluntarily assume all such risk. Denvered LLC, its officers, contractors, and suppliers disclaim all liability for injury, loss, or damage arising from your use of Reps to the maximum extent permitted by law (see our Terms of Service, Sections 14–16).

8. Not for minors without supervision

Reps is intended for users age 13 and older. Lifters under 18 should train under the supervision of a qualified adult.
