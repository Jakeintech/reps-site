Privacy Policy

Last updated November 10, 2026. Applies to the Reps app for iPhone and Apple Watch, its widgets and Live Activities, and this website.

The short version. Reps has no account and we run no servers. The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code, and no third-party software kits. Your workouts are saved on your device and in Apple Health. We never receive them.

On the App Store, Reps is labeled Data Not Collected.

1. Who we are

Reps is made by Denvered LLC, based in Colorado, United States ("Denvered", "we", "us"). For any privacy question or request, email privacy@denvered.com.

2. What we collect

Nothing. We do not operate user accounts, servers, databases or analytics. We do not receive your name, email address, Apple Account, device identifiers, location, workouts or health data. We do not sell, rent or share personal information, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

If you email us, we receive what you choose to send (your email address and message) and use it only to reply. We delete support email when it's no longer needed to help you.

3. What the app uses on your device

Reps works with the data below so its features can work. All of it stays on your iPhone and Apple Watch, or in Apple's own services that you control. Each permission is optional, asked for when you first use the feature, and can be turned off in the Settings app.

4. Apple Health

Health data that Reps reads or writes is used only to provide Reps features to you. It is never used for advertising or marketing, never sold, never shared with third parties and never used for data mining. You choose which types Reps can read and write in Settings, Privacy & Security, Health, Reps. If Apple Health syncs your Health data between your devices through iCloud, that's Apple's service, covered by Apple's privacy policy.

5. Sharing

We don't share your data because we don't have it. Things you choose to share, such as a share card you send in Messages or a file you export, go where you send them and are covered by that service's terms.

6. How long data is kept, and how to delete it

Data in the app stays until you delete it or delete the app. Workouts saved to Apple Health stay in Apple Health until you delete them there. Step-by-step instructions are on Delete Your Data.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU and UK, or state privacy laws in the US such as California's), you have rights to access, correct, export, restrict and delete your personal data. Because we hold none of your data, you can use these rights directly on your device: view and edit everything in Reps and the Health app, export your history from Reps Settings, and delete it as described above. You can still contact us at privacy@denvered.com and we'll answer within 30 days.

8. Children

Reps isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect anyone's personal data, including children's.

9. This website

This site has no cookies, analytics or tracking scripts. It's hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may log basic technical information such as IP addresses to run and secure its service, as described in GitHub's privacy statement. We don't receive or use those logs.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page. If a change means Reps would start collecting any data, we'll say so in the app before it happens.
