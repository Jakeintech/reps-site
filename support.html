Reps Support

Help, frequently asked questions, and how to reach us.

Getting started

How do I start a workout?

Open Reps and tap Start Workout, or tap your iPhone to any gym machine's NFC chip. If the chip is unknown, Reps asks you once to identify it — every future tap resolves instantly.

Do I need an account?

No. Reps has no accounts and no servers. Everything stays on your device. HealthKit provides continuity across your iPhone, Apple Watch, and other devices via iCloud Health — that's Apple's infrastructure, not ours.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone XS or newer running iOS 18+ for full logging, NFC, HealthKit, widgets, and Watch. iPhone 12 and newer add 2D pose, bar path, tempo, and Live Activities. iPhone 15 Pro and newer running iOS 26 add 3D pose analysis and Foundation Models coaching. Apple Watch Series 6+ running watchOS 11+.

NFC tap-to-log

Does this work with my gym's machines?

Most commercial gym equipment — Technogym, Life Fitness, EGYM, and others — already ships with passive NFC chips. Reps reads the chip's UID and maps it to a machine or exercise after your first tap. No writing to the chip, no changes to the equipment.

What about my home rack?

The Reps Starter Pack includes 20 pre-programmed NFC stickers. Or program your own using the in-app "Program sticker" tool in Settings.

Form analysis

Is my video uploaded anywhere?

No. Video capture, pose analysis, bar-path tracking, and coaching notes happen entirely on your device. By default, video is not saved — only the extracted metrics are kept. You can opt in to saving the video to the app's private sandbox in Settings.

Why is form analysis not available on my phone?

3D pose analysis requires an iPhone 15 Pro or newer. 2D pose and bar path work on iPhone 12 and newer. Older devices still get motion-based rep counting and numeric feedback.

HealthKit

What does Reps write to Health?

Each workout is saved as an HKWorkout of type Traditional Strength Training. Each exercise within the workout is saved as an HKWorkoutActivity sub-activity with custom metadata (sets, reps, weight, tempo, RPE, 1RM estimate, form score if analyzed). Heart rate series and energy burned during sets are attached.

What does Reps read from Health?

Workouts, heart rate, HRV, mobility/gait metrics (walking speed, asymmetry, stair speed), sleep stages, body metrics, and — only with your explicit opt-in — menstrual cycle data for periodization suggestions. All processing happens on your device.

How do I revoke health access?

iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Health > Reps. Toggle off any data type. Reps continues to work; features that depend on that data type will degrade gracefully.

Subscription

What does the free tier include?

Unlimited workouts, routines, history, custom exercises, plate calculator, rest timer, 1RM estimator, CSV export, HealthKit read/write, Apple Watch app, widgets, NFC tap-to-log, Live Activities, and share cards. The notebook is free.

What does Reps Pro unlock?

On-device Foundation Models coaching notes, natural-language workout builder, full video form analysis with bar-path overlay, machine OCR, Mobility × Strength correlation, and natural-language search over your history. The coach is Pro.

How do I cancel?

Settings > [Your Name] > Subscriptions on your iPhone, or open Reps and tap Manage Subscription. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period.

How do I request a refund?

All purchases are processed by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Data & deletion

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app. All local data is removed. Data written to HealthKit is yours to manage in the Health app: Browse > Workouts > tap a workout > Delete, or use "Delete All Data from Reps" in Privacy & Security > Health > Reps. See the Data Deletion page.
