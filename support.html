Getting started

How do I start a workout?

On the Train tab, tap Start next to any routine. Each lift opens on the weight and reps you did last time. Tap Log Set, or drag the big number to change the weight first.

Do I need an account?

No. Reps has no sign-up. Open it and start lifting.

Should I allow Apple Health?

Yes, if you want your workouts kept. Reps saves every finished workout to Apple Health, and reads your history from there. You can still log without it, and you can change access any time in Settings, Privacy & Security, Health, Reps.

How do I follow a program?

Reps includes ten programs, from beginner starters to Push/Pull/Legs, GZCLP, 5/3/1 Lite and nSuns Lite. Add one from the Train tab, then start its next day. You can also build your own routine from the exercise list.

Stickers

What stickers work?

Blank tap stickers sold online, often listed as NTAG213 or NTAG215. You need an iPhone that can read them (iPhone XS or later).

How do I set one up?

In Reps Settings, open Stickers, tap Program an NFC sticker, and pick its exercise. Put it on your rack, bench or machine. Next time, hold the top of your iPhone near it and that exercise opens.

A sticker opens the wrong exercise or nothing happens

Hold the top edge of your iPhone flat against the sticker for a second. If it still opens the wrong lift, set it up again from Reps Settings. Metal surfaces can block some stickers; a sticker made for metal, or a spot on plastic or a label, works better.

Apple Watch

How do I get Reps on my watch?

The watch app installs with the iPhone app. If it doesn't appear, open the Watch app on your iPhone, scroll to Available Apps and tap Install next to Reps.

Can I start on my phone and finish on my watch?

Yes. Start a workout on iPhone and the watch picks it up. It's saved as one workout, not two.

Siri, Shortcuts and widgets

What can I say to Siri?
  • "Start Push in Reps" (use any routine's name)
  • "Start workout in Reps"
  • "Repeat last set in Reps"
  • "End workout in Reps"
How do I put a routine on the Action Button?

In the Settings app, open Action Button, choose Shortcut, and pick a Reps action such as Start Routine.

Where are the widgets?

Lock Screen widgets show your next set and your streak. In Control Center, add the Start Workout control. While a workout runs, the rest timer shows on your Lock Screen, in the Dynamic Island and in StandBy.

Import, export and your data

How do I import from Strong or Hevy?

Export your workouts as a CSV file from the other app, save it to Files, then open Reps Settings and choose Import from Strong or Hevy. Your past workouts appear in your history and in Apple Health.

How do I export my history?

In Reps Settings, choose Export all sets to CSV to save a spreadsheet file of every workout.

Do my workouts move to a new iPhone?

Workouts live in Apple Health, which moves with your iPhone backup or iCloud. Routines and settings that live only in Reps move with an iPhone backup.

How do I delete my data?

See Delete Your Data. In short: delete the app to remove everything Reps keeps, and delete workouts in the Health app if you want those gone too.

Reps Pro

What's free and what's Pro?

Logging, routines, programs, history, the Apple Watch app, widgets, stickers, import and export are free with no limits. Reps Pro adds a weekly recap, Strength for life trends, Build by asking, Scan any machine and Form check (beta). Build by asking needs an iPhone with Apple Intelligence.

How do I cancel?

In the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Reps, then Cancel Subscription. Or open Reps Settings and tap Manage Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before your renewal date to avoid the next charge. Pro stays on until the end of the period you paid for.

I paid but Pro isn't unlocked

Open Reps Settings and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in to the App Store with the Apple Account you used to buy.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles all payments, so refunds go through Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Devices

Reps runs on iPhone with iOS 18 or later and Apple Watch with watchOS 11 or later. It isn't available for iPad or Mac.
