Data Deletion

Last updated: April 18, 2026

Reps has no account and no server. There is no remote user record to delete because none exists. All your data is on your device, in HealthKit, or in Apple's private iCloud Health store — which only you and Apple can access.

1. Delete app-local data

To remove every piece of data Reps stores on your device — exercise library, routines, cached coaching notes, ML embeddings for machine recognition, saved videos, and app preferences — delete the app:

  1. On your Home Screen, long-press the Reps icon.
  2. Tap Remove App.
  3. Tap Delete App to confirm.

All local data in the app's sandbox and App Group container is removed by iOS immediately. We cannot recover it and neither can Apple.

2. Delete Health data written by Reps

Workouts Reps wrote to HealthKit belong to you, not to us. To delete them:

  1. Open the Health app.
  2. Go to Browse > Workouts.
  3. Tap a workout, then Edit > Delete Workout, or swipe to delete.

To revoke Reps' access to HealthKit entirely:

  1. Settings > Privacy & Security > Health > Reps.
  2. Toggle off each category, or tap Turn Off All.
  3. To also remove all data Reps previously wrote, tap Delete All Data from Reps.

3. Delete subscription records

Your subscription is managed by Apple through your Apple ID, not by us. We never receive your payment information, name, or email. To cancel or view subscription history:

  1. Settings > [Your Name] > Subscriptions.
  2. Tap Reps, then Cancel Subscription.

Past transactions are retained by Apple per Apple's policies. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

4. What we cannot delete (because we never had it)

  • We do not operate user accounts.
  • We do not store your name, email, phone, Apple ID, device identifier, IP address, or location on any server.
  • We do not run analytics, attribution, advertising, or crash-reporting services.
  • We do not transmit your training data, videos, audio, or HealthKit data anywhere.

5. GDPR / CCPA / CPRA / VCDPA rights

You have the right to access, correct, port, restrict, and delete your personal data under applicable privacy laws. Because we do not collect personal data on any server, these rights are fully satisfied by the steps above:

  • Access — all your data is visible in-app or in the Health app.
  • Portability — export your workout history as CSV from Reps > Settings > Export.
  • Rectification — edit any workout in-app or in the Health app.
  • Erasure — follow steps 1 and 2 above.
  • Restriction / objection — disable any permission in iOS Settings; the related feature degrades, the rest of the app continues.

6. Questions
