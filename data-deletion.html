Delete Your Data

Last updated November 10, 2026

Reps has no account and no server, so there's no record about you for us to delete. Everything lives on your iPhone and Apple Watch, or in Apple Health. You can remove all of it yourself in a minute.

1. Delete what the app keeps

Routines, custom exercises, sticker setups, saved Form check clips, cached recaps and settings are removed when you delete the app:

  1. Touch and hold the Reps icon on your Home Screen.
  2. Tap Remove App, then Delete App.

Deleting the iPhone app also removes the Apple Watch app.

2. Delete workouts saved to Apple Health

Workouts Reps saved to Apple Health belong to you and stay there after you delete Reps. To delete some of them, open the Health app, go to Browse, Activity, Workouts, and swipe to delete a workout.

To delete every workout Reps saved and stop its access:

  1. Open the Settings app, then Privacy & Security, Health, Reps.
  2. Tap Delete All Data from "Reps".
  3. Turn off any permissions you no longer want to give.

3. Subscription records

Apple handles Reps Pro purchases. We never receive your name, email or payment details. To cancel, open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Reps. Apple keeps its own purchase records under Apple's privacy policy.

4. Keep a copy first

Before you delete anything, you can save your history: in Reps Settings, choose Export all sets to CSV.

5. Questions

Email privacy@denvered.com. We reply within 30 days, and usually much sooner.
